## 2. Devpost Text Description

### 🌟 Inspiration
Everyone has experienced that knot in their stomach before a viva exam, a grueling tech interview, asking a manager for a raise, or giving uncomfortable constructive feedback to a defensive direct report. Reading prep guides doesn't fix anxiety — **speaking out loud under realistic pressure does**.

We built **Polaris AI** as the North Star guiding professionals and students through anxiety-inducing conversations. Instead of generic chatbot text, Polaris acts as a dynamic vocal sparring partner that talks to you, listens to your real spoken answers, evaluates your vocal poise and structure, and coaches you to mastery.

### 🚀 What It Does
1. **Dynamic Persona & Scenario Inference**:
   Users type anything into an open prompt (*"DBMS viva exam"*, *"Frontend React tech interview"*, *"Asking my manager for a raise"*, or *"Setting boundaries with a demanding executive"*). Powered by Gemini AI, Polaris interprets the prompt, creates a custom evaluator persona (e.g. *Prof. Arvind Rao*, *Director Sarah Chen*), and speaks the realistic opening question out loud.

2. **Full Voice-to-Voice Rehearsal Loop**:
   - **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Spoken prompts with natural pacing.
   - **SpeechRecognizer**: Listens to user speech in real-time with an animated live voice waveform visualizer.
   - **Adaptive Multi-Turn Dialogue**: Up to 5 interactive exchanges. The AI reacts in-character, scores each response (0–10), and delivers a specific tactical tip before asking the follow-up question.
   - **Comprehensive Scorecard**: Final 0–10 score, readiness rating (*"Ready to Ace It"*, *"Almost Ready"*), key strengths, and actionable areas for improvement.

3. **Career Coaching & Manager Lab (Leadership Heather Category)**:
   A dedicated studio for new managers to practice difficult workplace conversations:
   - Delivering radical candor feedback to defensive engineers.
   - Holding firm boundaries when executives demand unscheduled weekend sprints.
   - Saying 'No' to out-of-scope feature creep while preserving trust.
   - Mediating bitter disputes between conflicting direct reports.
   - Delivering unexpected critical performance reviews.

4. **Local Room Database History & Trend Trajectory**:
   - Complete local offline database for all practice sessions.
   - Custom Canvas-rendered **Score Trajectory Line Chart** with interactive nodes and average score baseline.
   - Full transcript inspector to re-read and re-listen to previous answers.
